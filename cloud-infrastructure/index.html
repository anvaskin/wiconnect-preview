FAQ

Cloud questions, answered.

Book a cloud review
Should we choose Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace?

Both are excellent. The right choice depends on the applications you rely on, how your team works and what your clients use. We support both and will give you an honest recommendation — including staying where you are, if that’s best.

Will migrating to the cloud disrupt our work?

A well-planned migration shouldn’t. We move data in stages, test before cut-over, schedule changes outside business hours where needed and support your team on the first days after the move.

Isn’t Microsoft 365 or Google already backing up our data?

These platforms keep services available, but they are not a complete backup of your data against deletion, ransomware or account compromise. We recommend independent backups for critical information.

Can we keep our phone numbers if we switch to VoIP?

In most cases, yes — existing numbers can usually be transferred to a new hosted phone system. We confirm this during planning.
